Account & Data Deletion

AI English Coach

Developer: NES06RA

This page explains how users of AI English Coach can request deletion of their account and associated application data.

How to request deletion

To request deletion of your AI English Coach account and associated data, send an email to:

Request Account Deletion

Please send the request from the email address associated with your AI English Coach account.

Use the following subject line:

AI English Coach — Account Deletion Request

If you cannot send the request from the email address associated with your account, please provide enough information for us to identify and verify the account securely.

What will be deleted

When an account deletion request is completed, NES06RA deletes the user's account and associated application data maintained by AI English Coach.

This may include account information, authentication information, conversation history, messages, learning and personalization data, and other information associated with the account.

Data retention

Certain information may be retained where necessary or permitted for legitimate security, fraud prevention, legal, or regulatory purposes. Any such retention will be handled in accordance with our Privacy Policy and applicable law.

In-app deletion

Users who have access to AI English Coach can also initiate account deletion directly from the application using the account deletion functionality.

Privacy Policy

For more information about how AI English Coach processes personal information, please review our Privacy Policy .
