1. Introduction

AI English Coach is an English language learning application developed and operated by NES06RA.

This Privacy Policy explains how AI English Coach accesses, collects, uses, stores, and shares information when you use the application.

We aim to collect and process only the information necessary to provide the application's core functionality, including authentication, conversation history, speech transcription, AI-powered English coaching, and personalized learning.

2. Information We Collect

2.1 Account Information

When you sign in using Google Sign-In, the application may receive and store information associated with your Google account, including:

  • name;
  • email address;
  • profile picture;
  • Google account identifier.

This information is used to create, identify, and authenticate your AI English Coach account.

2.2 Authentication Information

AI English Coach uses authentication information, including application authentication tokens, to maintain your signed-in session and securely access your account.

2.3 Conversation Data

When you use the AI English Coach, we process information necessary to provide the conversation and coaching service, including:

  • messages submitted by you;
  • AI-generated responses;
  • conversation identifiers;
  • conversation timestamps;
  • conversation history.

Conversation data is used to provide the coaching service, maintain conversation history, and provide continuity between sessions.

2.4 Voice and Speech Data

When you use the speaking practice functionality, AI English Coach requests access to your device microphone and records audio after you grant microphone permission.

The recorded audio is transmitted to our backend service for speech-to-text processing. The resulting transcription is used to provide English language coaching.

Microphone access is initiated by the user through the speaking practice functionality. The application does not continuously record audio in the background.

2.5 Learning and Progress Data

AI English Coach may store information generated from your learning activity in order to personalize the coaching experience.

This may include recurring language patterns, detected mistakes, practice targets, and other learning-related information used to adapt future practice.

2.6 Technical and Usage Information

The application and its backend may process technical information necessary to operate, secure, troubleshoot, and maintain the service.

This may include authentication status, request information, error information, and usage information associated with service limits and abuse prevention.

3. How We Use Information

We use information collected through AI English Coach for the following purposes:

  • to authenticate users;
  • to provide English language coaching;
  • to transcribe speech;
  • to generate AI-powered responses;
  • to generate speech responses;
  • to maintain conversation history;
  • to personalize learning and practice;
  • to maintain and secure the service;
  • to prevent abuse and enforce usage limits;
  • to respond to support and privacy requests.

We do not sell personal information.

4. Third-Party Service Providers

AI English Coach relies on third-party service providers to provide certain application functionality.

Google

Google Sign-In is used for authentication. Information associated with your Google account may be received and processed for account creation and authentication.

OpenAI

OpenAI services are used to provide AI-powered functionality, including speech transcription, English coaching responses, and text-to-speech functionality.

Information necessary to provide these features may be transmitted to OpenAI for processing.

Railway

Railway is used to host application backend infrastructure and database services. Account, conversation, learning, and related application data may be stored on this infrastructure.

Third-party providers process information according to their applicable terms and privacy practices.

5. Data Security

We use reasonable technical and organizational measures designed to protect user information against unauthorized access, alteration, disclosure, or destruction.

Communications between the mobile application and our backend services are protected using HTTPS/TLS.

Authentication-protected application endpoints require valid user authentication.

No method of electronic transmission or storage can be guaranteed to be completely secure. We therefore cannot guarantee absolute security of information.

6. Data Retention

We retain account, conversation, learning, and related application data for as long as necessary to provide the service and maintain the user's account.

When an account deletion request is completed, we delete the user's account and associated application data from our application systems, subject to information that may need to be retained where permitted or required for legitimate security, fraud prevention, legal, or regulatory purposes.

7. Account and Data Deletion

AI English Coach provides users with an account deletion option within the application.

Users can also request deletion of their account and associated application data through our external account deletion page:

AI English Coach — Account & Data Deletion

Users may also contact us at nes06ra@gmail.com to request account and data deletion.

When a deletion request is completed, we delete the account and associated application data maintained by our application systems.

Where third-party service providers process information on our behalf, we take appropriate steps to address deletion of applicable data in accordance with our agreements and their applicable data handling practices.

8. Children's Privacy

AI English Coach is not specifically directed toward children under the age of 13.

We do not knowingly collect personal information from children under 13 without appropriate authorization.

If you believe that a child has provided personal information through the application, please contact us at nes06ra@gmail.com.

9. Microphone Permission

AI English Coach requests microphone access only when the user chooses to use the speaking practice functionality.

Microphone access is used to record the user's speech for speech-to-text processing and English language coaching.

The application does not use the microphone for continuous background recording.

10. Changes to This Privacy Policy

We may update this Privacy Policy when the application's functionality, data practices, or applicable requirements change.

The updated version will be published at this URL and the "Last updated" date will be revised accordingly.
